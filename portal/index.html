Client portal

Your client portal

Each client signs in on your domain — for example portal.yourbrand.com — with a one-time code. They can review calls, recordings, transcripts and usage, under your brand.

What your client gets

The same branded space for every client, on your domain.

  • Your brand

    White-label login and dashboard: your logo, colours and a portal on your own domain.

  • One-time sign-in

    Clients get a code by email. There is no password to remember.

  • Results emails

    An automatic recap of what the agent handled, sent every week or month.

  • Hours and coverage

    See after-hours conversations and when customers typically call.

  • Staff-time value

    Optionally show what the handled calls would have cost in staff time.

  • Phone numbers

    See which agent answers each number.

A look inside

What the client sees

Calls, transcripts and usage in one place, with 7, 14 or 30 day views.

Screenshots from our demo client.

A clear picture of the week

  • Calls and chats, daily volume, and 7, 14 or 30 day views
  • Completed rate, after-hours conversations, average length and staff time handled
  • Call outcomes and a heatmap of when customers reach out
  • Recent conversations at a glance

Every call in one list

  • Totals for calls, staff time, completion rate and peak hours
  • Search by phone number, contact or conversation, and filter the list
  • Date, channel, duration, agent, from, to, direction and end reason
  • Open any row to play the recording and read the transcript

How each agent is doing

  • Calls in the last 7 days, completion rate and average duration
  • Staff time handled, after-hours volume and last active time
  • Per-agent charts for volume and time
  • A detailed performance view for a single agent

Trends you can check

  • Filter by channel
  • Conversations by channel, sentiment, volume and outcomes
  • Peak-time heatmap and distribution by hour
  • Call status at a glance

The call itself

  • Play or download the recording
  • Full transcript with search, copy and download
  • An AI summary, plus actions the agent took, such as knowledge-base lookups
  • Call analysis, response time and conversation flow
