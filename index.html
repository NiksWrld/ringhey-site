How it works

From first call to go-live in three steps

We handle the setup, voice and integrations. You keep control of what the agent says.

  1. 1

    Discover

    We map your calls, FAQs and goals, then collect your knowledge: website, documents, prices and rules.

  2. 2

    Build & tune

    We design the conversation, pick a natural voice and connect calendars or CRM. You test it until it sounds right.

  3. 3

    Go live & improve

    Your agent answers your number or website. We monitor calls and keep improving answers every month.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Does it sound like a robot?

No. We use state-of-the-art neural voices with natural pacing. Callers can interrupt and the agent responds within about a second.

Can it use my existing phone number?

Yes. You can forward your number to the agent all the time or only when your team doesn't pick up, or we provide a new number.

What happens if the agent can't answer?

It says so honestly, takes a message or transfers the call to a person. It only answers from information you've approved.

Which languages are supported?

English and German out of the box, and many more on request. The agent can detect the caller's language.

How long does setup take?

A focused first agent is usually live in two to four weeks, depending on integrations and how quickly content is ready.

What about privacy?

Callers are told they're speaking with an AI assistant. Recordings and transcripts are only kept as long as you choose, and we sign a data processing agreement.
