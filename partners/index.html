What you earn

A worked example, on Growth

Commission is 10% of what the referred customer actually pays in their first 12 months — setup and monthly. The code only reduces the one-time setup, not the monthly plan.

What your referral gets

On Growth ($990 setup + $590/month, 500 minutes), they get $99 off setup and pay $891 to get started, then $590 a month.

Starter is $49 off setup ($490 → $441), then $290 a month. Solo is $9.90 off setup ($99 → $89.10), then $129 a month.

What you earn

10% of $891 setup is $89.10. Then $59 a month for 12 months ($708). First-year total: $797.10, if they stay on Growth.

On Starter that’s $44.10 on setup plus $29 a month for 12 months, $392.10 in the first year. On Solo, $8.91 on setup plus $12.90 a month for 12 months, $163.71 in the first year.

We pay commission monthly by bank transfer or PayPal. Custom packages are quoted separately — talk to us if you’re referring a larger project.

FAQ

Questions, answered

When do I get paid?

Monthly, by bank transfer or PayPal, on commission from payments we actually received that month. That covers the referred customer’s setup and their monthly plan during the first 12 months.

How do you track referrals?

Each partner gets a personal Stripe promotion code. The customer enters it at checkout. That’s how we tie the sale to you and apply their setup discount.

Does it cost anything to join?

No. There’s no fee to become a partner and no minimum number of referrals.

Can I resell Ringhey under my brand?

This program is a referral arrangement, not a white-label or reseller licence. If you want to offer the agent under your own brand, talk to us.

What does the customer pay?

With your code, $99 off Growth setup, $49 off Starter, or $9.90 off Solo. Monthly plans stay at the list price. Extra minutes are billed at cost-plus, as on our pricing page, and are included in the “everything they pay” commission for those first 12 months.
